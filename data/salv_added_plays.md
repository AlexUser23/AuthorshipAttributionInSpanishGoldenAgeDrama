# Plays added to the SALV corpus

The original corpus contains 73 text files and the expanded corpus contains 85. The 12 additions below are the filename set difference `salv_expanded_corpus/` minus `salv_original_corpus/`. All original filenames are present in the expanded corpus. Titles retain the spelling of the filenames; author labels also come from filenames.

The added plays come from ETSO (Cuéllar and Vega García-Luengos, 2017–2026), as confirmed by the author, and were selected using textual integrity criteria. Per-play editions and reuse conditions remain to be specified.
